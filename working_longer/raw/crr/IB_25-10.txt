Conclusion
The good news is that, prior to COVID, the trend over
the last 20 years showed a dramatic decline in the
percentage of 62-year-olds claiming benefits as soon
as they became eligible, and that trend continued
through COVID. As a result, the average claiming
age has increased, although both the birth-year data
and claim-age data show an increase that falls short of
the three-year increase in the average retirement age.
