    Cost of Care Survey regions are based on Metropolitan
    Statistical Areas (MSAs) as defined by the Office of
    Management and Budget (OMB) (census.gov).

    In July 2023, the OMB refined its MSA delineations,
    thereby impacting some of our region definitions. All 2025
    Cost of Care data reflects survey results based on the
    new delineations, which can often include counties from
    other nearby states.
